# Paddle domain-review appeal draft

Use this only after the revised application has been deployed to
`app.outlio.io` and that subdomain has been submitted for review. Keep every
statement accurate; do not imply that Outlio has stopped offering a separate
service if it has not.

---

Subject: Request for reconsideration — app.outlio.io standalone SaaS product

Hello Paddle Risk Team,

Thank you for reviewing our original domain. I understand why outlio.io was
classified as advertising or direct-marketing services: that domain describes
a separate human-delivered agency offering.

I am asking you to review `app.outlio.io`, which is the dedicated domain for a
different product: Outlio Lead Engine, a standalone, self-serve B2B SaaS
application.

Paddle would be used exclusively to sell subscriptions to this software. A
Lead Engine subscription does not purchase or include advertising, managed
marketing, appointment setting, consulting, telemarketing, messaging, or any
other human-delivered service. Our separately contracted agency work will not
be sold, invoiced, or processed through Paddle.

The software lets a customer upload a file already saved on their own computer
and converts the information in that file into a structured CSV. It does not
send emails or messages, run campaigns, automate social-media engagement,
place telephone calls, or provide an advertising service. It does not ask for
or use LinkedIn credentials, cookies, or sessions, and it does not sign in to
or navigate LinkedIn on a customer's behalf.

We revised the dedicated product domain so the product description, included
features, localized pricing, Lead Engine Terms, Privacy Policy, and Refund
Policy are clear and directly accessible. We also state explicitly that Paddle
checkout is only for the self-serve software subscription.

Please reconsider the dedicated software domain `app.outlio.io`. I can provide
a reviewer test account and a product walkthrough if helpful.

Kind regards,

Muhammad Husnain
Outlio
